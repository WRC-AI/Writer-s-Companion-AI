Expand

Enrich your existing prose using current-chapter context.

Expand commands the Relevance Engine to zoom in on your active workspace. It takes a scene, a beat, or an emotional moment you have already written and uses localized scoring to grow it with precision.

  • Zero Architectural Drift — it does not invent new plotlines or add conversational noise; it illuminates the richness already living in your shot.

Expand doesn't drift. It doesn't add noise. It amplifies the truth of the moment.

The blueprint is your chapter's map — not a summary written after the fact, but a living record your AI partner consults before it ever writes a word. It informs what the AI writes next — it never rewrites what you've already written.

The blueprint is what every Blue Sky mode reads before it touches your story — the record that makes precise, targeted analysis possible in the first place.

Structured Analysis Format

The blueprint is the chapter's analytical backbone — a structured, explicit record of what the story has established, what it is building toward, and what remains unresolved. It is not a summary. It is not an interpretation. It is a factual ledger of narrative commitments.

Every blueprint entry is written in a controlled format so the AI can evaluate continuity, character logic, emotional trajectory, and unresolved obligations without guessing. Nothing is inferred. Nothing is assumed. Every detail must be stated directly.

A chapter's blueprint contains:

  • Established Facts — concrete story elements introduced in the chapter
  • Active Trajectories — arcs, tensions, or setups that must continue
  • Unresolved Obligations — promises the chapter makes that future chapters must honor
  • Constraints — rules, limitations, or conditions the story must not violate

The blueprint is updated only through explicit additions or revisions. No entry is ever overwritten. No entry is ever silently replaced.

Blueprint as Living Record

The blueprint is not written after the chapter — it is the record the AI consults before it writes a single word.

Every addition to the blueprint is stored as its own entry — informing what the AI writes next, never quietly altering what came before.

This allows the AI to:

  • Maintain continuity across chapters
  • Track character development without drift
  • Honor unresolved obligations
  • Prevent contradictions
  • Understand the story's internal logic before generating text

Your original chapter text is always preserved. Blueprint entries never mutate the manuscript — they only inform it.
