How Pre-Beta Works

There is no cost to take part.

We're looking for a small number of writers to try WRC on their own manuscripts. Tell us a little about yourself below. We'll review every response and invite a few people to the next step.

The next step is a sample evaluation. If we invite you, we'll ask for a couple of chapters from a work in progress or a completed manuscript. I'll run them through WRC and send you the analysis, so you can see whether WRC is useful for your writing. If you like what you see, I'll tell you more about the WRC beta and what it involves. Please don't send any chapters now. You'll get instructions if we invite you.

This works both ways. You decide whether WRC is worth your time, and we look for writers with a diverse range of backgrounds and writing processes who might be a good fit.

A Note About Timing

My wife is currently battling Stage 4 cancer, and her care and well-being will always be my highest priority. When she's able, she'll help me choose who we invite. WRC is a passion project that we work on when time and conditions allow, so please don't expect quick replies. Some may take days or even weeks. I would rather say so now than set expectations I can't meet. If you're comfortable with that pace, we'd be glad to hear from you.

Your Manuscript
