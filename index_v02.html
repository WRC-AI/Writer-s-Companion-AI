The Foundation

Blueprint Entries

Every chapter carries its own set of blueprint entries: a wealth of insights for your review, and the foundation that powers Manuscript Intelligence.

Blueprint entries capture:

  • Storyline: the living threads of your manuscript. They vanish and resurface, collide and converge, and build toward the climax.
  • Characters: who appears, what drives them, how they change, and the thread that links every chapter they appear in.
  • Continuity: the facts, timelines, open questions, and other threads that hold the story together, along with what works and what could be stronger.

Manuscript Intelligence

Relevance Engine

Every chapter has its own storylines and characters. When you work on one, the Relevance Engine follows those threads through everything you've written, weighs how closely each earlier chapter connects, and selects the most relevant ones.

Blueprint

Every chapter carries a comprehensive set of blueprint entries, each providing a full editorial read. Each entry names what's there, judges how well it works, and suggests one way to strengthen it. Entries also look for what you might have missed: the detail that foreshadows, the thread left loose, the emotion beneath a character's reaction. They summarize, evaluate, describe, and suggest. They never rewrite your story. Ignore what you like, or use them as creative stepping stones. It's your decision.

Together, they show an intricate tapestry of your chapter.

Backstory

Blueprint entries of selected chapters become the backstory that forms the foundation necessary for an insightful analysis that reflects the nuances of your manuscript, not just the chapter in front of you.

Analysis

Focused on what matters to you

Choose the storylines and characters you care about: a protagonist and antagonist, a love triangle, a subplot and the characters tangled in it. Then tell WRC what matters most. Pick from a menu of focuses like Hopeful Resolution, Dark Psychological, or Cliffhangers, or write your own.

WRC looks across every chapter and gives your AI relevant backstory for the ones that matter most to what you've chosen. What comes back is an analysis of how a storyline progresses, or how your characters' conflicts intertwine, across the whole manuscript.

Want to see your manuscript through an entirely different lens? Rerun it with a different combination of focuses, characters, and/or storylines. Replace or delete any analysis, or save and use whatever you want, independently.

Why WRC Works

WRC doesn't ask your AI to remember your manuscript. It does the remembering, and hands your AI exactly what it needs.

At the center of it all is the WRC Relevance Engine™, the system that:

  • maintains comprehensive and insightful blueprint entries for every chapter you've written
  • determines which chapters genuinely matter to the one you're working on now
  • recognizes recurring characters and storylines the way an attentive reader would
  • grounds every AI response in the correct parts of your story, automatically

About Your Data

Your Story. Your Data. Always.

Writer's Companion is entirely self-contained — your manuscript lives in a standard SQL database, not on someone else's servers, not inside a cloud AI service. Your data belongs to you. WRC makes no claim to it.
