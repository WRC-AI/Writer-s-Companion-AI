The Foundation

Blueprint Entries

Every chapter carries its own blueprint — a structured, explicit record of what the story has established, what it's building toward, and what still needs to be resolved.

The blueprint is your chapter's map — not a summary written after the fact, but a living record your AI partner consults before it ever writes a word. It informs what the AI writes next — it never rewrites what you've already written.

[DRAFT] When you work on a new chapter, WRC gathers the blueprint entries of the chapters related to it and hands them to your AI as backstory — the heart and soul of your manuscript, delivered exactly when it's needed.

Author Control

Deterministic Workflow

This is the pillar that makes WRC different from every other writing tool.

Author Control is a deterministic workflow — a system that ensures you remain the architect of your manuscript at every step. No AI drift. No tonal contamination. No accidental rewrites of your voice.

Every interaction is structured. Every suggestion is contextual. Every transformation is reversible.

It keeps your intent at the center of the creative process. It ensures that the manuscript evolves according to your rules, your canon, your emotional arc, and your long-term vision.

You are not handing your story to an AI. You are directing it.

WRC is built like an instrument — precise, deterministic, and built for authors who architect entire worlds.

Every AI change is tracked with full redline comparison against what you originally sent — see exactly what changed, line by line, and accept or reject each one. Nothing is applied to your manuscript without your review.

[DRAFT] Trace — Blueprint Mapping. Right-click any blueprint entry and see every chapter where it appears, scored by how closely each one relates to the chapter you're working in.

About Your Data

Your Story. Your Data. Always.

Writer's Companion is entirely self-contained — your manuscript lives in a standard SQL database, not on someone else's servers, not inside a cloud AI service. Your data belongs to you. WRC makes no claim to it.

  • Every request WRC sends to an AI application is independent — a fresh conversation, every time, with no memory carried between interactions. WRC does the remembering for it: it hands your AI the specific backstory that actually matters for that moment — nothing more, nothing less. No AI application trains on your manuscript, and none retains anything after the request completes. WRC is the memory the AI itself is incapable of keeping.
  • Bring your work with you. Already deep into a manuscript? Import an existing work-in-progress — no special formatting or chapter headers required — and pick up right where you left off.
  • Take it with you when you leave. Export your entire project back out to a Word document, ready for the next stage of your process.
