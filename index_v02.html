The Foundation

Blueprint Entries

Every chapter carries its own blueprint — a structured, explicit record of what the story has established, what it's building toward, and what still needs to be resolved.

The blueprint is not a summary written after the fact. It is a living record of your chapter, and it is what WRC later hands to your AI as backstory. [DRAFT: verify each item below against what a Blueprint Entry actually contains]

  • Storyline — what happens, and what it sets in motion.
  • Characters — who appears, and how they act and change.
  • Continuity — the facts, timelines, and constraints the chapter establishes.
  • Open threads and revision opportunities — what the chapter still owes the reader, and where it could be stronger.

You choose the level of analysis you want, from light polish to significant revision.

Who WRC Is For

Serious About Your Craft

WRC is built for authors who are serious about their craft — the kind of writer already juggling character sheets, timeline notes, and a dozen open questions about their manuscript. It rewards that seriousness with real depth.

A quick technical note: WRC works alongside your AI application, not inside it, so you'll be comfortable moving between two windows and copying text back and forth between them. If that's a normal part of how you already work, you'll feel right at home.

About Your Data

Your Story. Your Data. Always.

Writer's Companion is entirely self-contained — your manuscript lives in a standard SQL database, not on someone else's servers, not inside a cloud AI service. Your data belongs to you. WRC makes no claim to it.

  • Begin a fresh AI conversation for every request. WRC supplies exactly the backstory each one needs — the AI interaction performs best without leftovers from earlier exchanges.
  • Bring your work with you. Already deep into a manuscript? Import an existing work-in-progress — no special formatting or chapter headers required — and pick up right where you left off.
  • Take it with you when you leave. Export your entire project back out to a Word document, ready for the next stage of your process.
