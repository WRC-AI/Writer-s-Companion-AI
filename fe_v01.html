Blueprint Architecture

Structured Analysis Format

Every chapter carries its own blueprint — a structured, explicit record of what the story has established, what it's building toward, and what still needs to be resolved. Nothing is inferred. Nothing is assumed.

The blueprint is your chapter's map — not a summary written after the fact, but a living record your AI partner consults before it ever writes a word. Every addition is stored separately. Every revision is labeled. Your original text is always preserved.

Why WRC Works

At the center of it all is the WRC Relevance Engine™ — the system that reads every chapter you've written and determines which ones genuinely matter to the one you're working on now. Not by matching keywords, but by recognizing your recurring characters and storylines the way an attentive reader would. Every AI response you receive is grounded in the right parts of your story, automatically.
