The Relevance Engine — The Foundation Beneath Blue Sky

Before Blue Sky, there is the WRC Relevance Engine™ — the system that gives Writer's Companion its architectural memory.

Most AI tools respond only to the text in front of them. WRC does something different. The Relevance Engine evaluates every chapter in your manuscript and determines which ones are most connected to the chapter you're working on — structurally, emotionally, thematically, or through character continuity. It identifies the chapters that matter, extracts their backstory, and feeds that information directly into your AI interface.

Context that spans your manuscript. Continuity that persists across chapters. Precision that reflects your actual story architecture.

Its true power emerges the moment you step into Blue Sky — where the Relevance Engine becomes the backbone for deep expansion, parallel development, and whole-manuscript analysis.

The Three Powers

Three modes. Three different kinds of momentum.
Deepen what exists. Branch into what might. See it all at once.

🌊

Expand — Deepen What Exists

Reads the chapter you already wrote and strengthens it from the inside out. Sharper emotional causality, cleaner pacing, richer connective tissue. Up to five new chapters — all anchored to your original intent.

🌀

Parallel — Branch Without Breaking

A new storyline built from the same foundation — new characters, new stakes, new pressure points — designed to collide with your main arc when the moment is right. Up to five new chapters, exploring without destabilizing what already works.

🧭

Narrative Analysis — See the Whole Manuscript

The deep dive. Evaluates your manuscript as a connected whole — every thread, every arc, every unresolved promise. It identifies what works (and how to strengthen it) and what doesn't (and how to fix it). The closest thing to an editor with perfect recall.
